Prices are end-of-day from the daily data. A stock that is not on the radar needs a price you enter, and its company flags show n/a. Upcoming results (board meeting) dates from NSE, usually about one month ahead. A company that is not listed here has no date announced yet (n/a). Dates can change. Price can gap up or down after results, so plan entries and stop-loss carefully.
Dates filter by the day a stock first appeared on the radar. If stop-loss and target are both touched on the same day, it is counted as a stop-loss (conservative). Pending = waiting for price to reach the entry. Expired = time window ended without hitting target or stop (shown at the window's last close). Tracking windows: Ultra 5 trading days, Short Term 20, New Listings 60, Multibagger and Quality 250 (about 1 year). Multibagger, Quality and New Listings tracking started in October 2026; stocks that appeared before that are not included. "Days" = trading days after the signal day. For stocks still Open, P/L % is the change up to the last close. Class applies to Ultra and Short Term only.

After a stop-loss hit: "After stop max %" = the highest rise from the stop price in the days after the stop. "Whipsaw" = Yes if the price later also touched the target (the stop may have been too tight), No if it did not, "-" if it is still unknown (not enough days observed yet). "Whipsaw %" = Yes out of (Yes + No); stops with unknown result are not counted. With only a few stops this number is not reliable, so wait for a few weeks of data. "On radar" = Yes if the stock is on today's list, Removed if it is not.
A "big mover" is a stock (price Rs 50+, average traded value Rs 10 crore+) whose close rose 15%+ within 5 trading days (Ultra) or 20 trading days (Short Term). Caught = it was on our list that day (or in the 2 days before). Missed = we had a snapshot and it was not listed. Unknown = no snapshot exists for that day (older dates), so the reason is only an approximation from price data. Date filter = the day the move started.
Data: Yahoo Finance end-of-day prices (via yfinance), scanned automatically after market close. Company details come from NSE and Yahoo Finance and can be missing or slightly delayed; "n/a" means unknown, not zero. Stocks on the NSE ASM or GSM lists are removed automatically.

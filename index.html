Market Health
Weekly scan (updated every Saturday). Quality companies with fast 3-year sales and profit growth, high ROCE, positive cash flow and a price that has not already run up. Meant for 1-3 years. Stop-loss is wide (20-25%), so the suggested quantity is small. Ranked by score; you pick the top few. Always verify ROCE and growth on screener.in before buying. Not checked: auditor changes and related-party transactions.
Dates filter by the day a stock first appeared on the radar. If stop-loss and target are both touched on the same day, it is counted as a stop-loss (conservative). Pending = waiting for price to reach the entry. Expired = time window ended without hitting target or stop (shown at the window's last close).
A "big mover" is a stock (price Rs 50+, average traded value Rs 10 crore+) whose close rose 15%+ within 5 trading days (Ultra) or 20 trading days (Short Term). Caught = it was on our list that day (or in the 2 days before). Missed = we had a snapshot and it was not listed. Unknown = no snapshot exists for that day (older dates), so the reason is only an approximation from price data. Date filter = the day the move started.
Data: Yahoo Finance end-of-day prices (via yfinance), scanned automatically after market close. Company details come from NSE and Yahoo Finance and can be missing or slightly delayed; "n/a" means unknown, not zero. Stocks on the NSE ASM or GSM lists are removed automatically. Scores are estimates, not guarantees. Always verify on your own chart. This is a research aid, not investment advice.
